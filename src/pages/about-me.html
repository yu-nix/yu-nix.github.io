Yu likes to draw cute and silly things. Yu built this site to have a place to display the cute and silly things. Yu likes other things too, but for now is focusing on cute, silly things and trying to draw cool and dense patterns.

Interests: simple stirring sketches, surface patterns, color schemes, visual media with cool art styles(off the top of my head Phonopolis, Metaphor:ReFantazio, Mononoke), crochet, big coffee table books with as little words as possible.

What I would like to get into: temari, riso printing(printing in general ex silk screen printing), playing video games(I look at them, but rarely do I find my self playing them :<), compilers, performance, techniques to find bugs.
